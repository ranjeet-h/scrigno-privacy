Scrigno Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Scrigno is an Android app that keeps your notes and secure records in an encrypted vault on your phone. This policy explains what happens to your information. In short: it stays on your phone, and the developer never receives it.

Information we collect

None. Scrigno does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information. There is no account and no server. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send data over a network. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or trackers.

Information stored on your device

The notes, records, images and settings you add are stored only on your phone, encrypted. The key that opens them is protected by your phone's secure hardware and by your fingerprint or your passcode. The developer cannot access your data or recover it for you.

Fingerprint

Fingerprint checks are performed by Android. Scrigno never receives or stores your fingerprint.

Backups you create

You can save an encrypted backup file to a location you choose using the Android file picker. The file goes only where you put it; Scrigno does not upload it anywhere.

Permissions

Scrigno requests only the fingerprint permissions Android requires for fingerprint unlock. It does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or notification permissions.

Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data.

Deleting your data

You can delete items inside the app, or uninstall Scrigno to remove everything it stores on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Scrigno is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and its date will be published on this page.
