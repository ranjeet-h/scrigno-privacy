Scrigno Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Scrigno is an Android app that keeps your notes and secure records in an encrypted vault on your phone. The developer does not receive your vault content or microphone audio. The app uses the internet only for optional voice-model downloads; the model host receives connection information as explained below. Feature availability depends on your app version and phone.

Information we collect

The developer receives no vault content, pictures or speech from Scrigno. There is no account, sign-up or developer backend. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service or trackers, and does not upload your notes, records, pictures or speech.

Information stored on your phone

Your notes, secure records, attached pictures and vault settings are encrypted in the vault on your phone, protected by your fingerprint or app passcode. The developer cannot access your vault or recover it for you. Downloaded speech-model files are stored separately in the app's private storage and are not part of your vault or backups.

Fingerprint

Fingerprint checks are performed by Android. Scrigno never receives, stores or sees your fingerprint.

Backups you create

You can save an encrypted backup to a location you choose using the Android file picker, including a drive app you already use. Scrigno does not upload backups itself; any transfer by your chosen storage provider is governed by that provider. Keep your backup and your recovery phrase safe. Scrigno does not send your recovery phrase anywhere.

Clipboard

When you copy a secret, Scrigno places it on the Android clipboard and asks Android to clear it after a short time. Other apps may be able to read clipboard content where Android allows it, and an app you paste into may keep what you paste.

Voice typing (optional)

On supported phones, Scrigno can turn your speech into note text using a model that runs on your phone. Scrigno does not save audio recordings or send speech to a server. Recognised text is saved as part of your note. The microphone is used only while you are using voice typing.

Voice-model downloads and connection information

Voice typing needs an optional download of public speech-model files from the Moonshine model host, over HTTPS. The host receives your IP address and the ordinary request information needed to serve the files. Scrigno sends no vault content, speech, account details or app identifier with that request. The host operates separately and its logging is outside Scrigno's control; we do not promise that it keeps no connection logs.

This is the app's only network use. Downloads begin only when you choose to download a voice model. After installation, voice typing works offline, and so do notes and secure records.

Scanning cards and documents (optional)

Scrigno uses your phone's camera app to photograph a page or card. Recognition runs on your phone and nothing is sent to a server. You review the proposed details and choose whether to save them. A photo is kept only if you choose to save it, and then it is encrypted in the vault. Your camera app may keep its own copy of the photo, outside Scrigno's control.

Permissions

Scrigno requests the fingerprint permissions Android requires. It requests microphone permission only when you tap the microphone for voice typing. It includes internet permission only for the optional voice-model download. Scrigno does not request location, contacts, camera, broad storage or notification permission.

Deleting your data

You can delete content inside Scrigno. Deleted notes stay in Recently Deleted for a limited time before they are removed permanently. Uninstalling Scrigno removes its app data and downloaded models. Backup files and recovery-phrase files you saved elsewhere remain there until you delete them, as do copies you shared or pasted into other apps.

Children

Scrigno is not intended for children under 18. The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through the app.
