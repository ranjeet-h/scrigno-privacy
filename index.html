Scrigno Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Scrigno is an Android app that keeps your notes and secure records in an encrypted vault on your phone. The developer does not receive your vault content or microphone audio. The app uses the internet only for optional voice-model downloads; the model host receives connection information as explained below. Feature availability depends on your app version and phone.

Information we collect

The developer receives no vault content, pictures or speech from Scrigno. There is no account, sign-up or developer backend. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting service or trackers, and does not upload your notes, records, pictures or speech. Optional voice-model downloads contact a separate model host, which receives the connection information described below.

Information stored on your phone

Your notes, secure records, attached pictures, search index and vault settings are encrypted in the vault on your phone. Public speech-model files and their model-selection and practice-test settings are stored separately in the app’s private storage; they are not part of the encrypted vault or its backups. The vault key is protected using Android’s secure hardware and your fingerprint or chosen app passcode. The developer cannot access your vault or recover it for you.

Fingerprint

Fingerprint checks are performed by Android. Scrigno never receives, stores or sees your fingerprint.

Backups you create

You can save an encrypted .svault backup to a location you choose using the Android file picker, including a drive app you already use. Scrigno writes the file to that chosen location; any transfer by your chosen storage provider is governed by that provider. Scrigno does not upload backups itself. Anyone with both the backup and your 24-word recovery phrase could open it, so keep them safe and separate. The app passcode alone cannot restore a backup on another phone. The recovery phrase is shown during setup and can be displayed again after a fresh identity check; Scrigno does not keep the words in readable form or send them anywhere.

Clipboard

When you copy a secret, Scrigno places it on the Android clipboard, marks it as sensitive and attempts to clear it after about 30 seconds. Other apps may access clipboard content where Android allows it. A recipient app may retain anything you paste into it.

Voice typing (optional)

On supported phones, Scrigno can turn your speech into note text using an on-device model. Audio is processed in memory; Scrigno does not save audio recordings or send speech to a server. Recognised text is saved as part of your note. The microphone is used only while voice typing is switched on, and capture stops when you stop voice typing, leave the note, lock the app or leave the app.

Voice-model downloads and connection information

Voice typing requires an optional download from download.moonshine.ai, the Moonshine speech-model host. The download uses HTTPS. The host receives your IP address and normal request information needed to serve the model files. Scrigno sends no vault content, speech, account details or app-generated identifier with that request. The model host operates separately; its server logging and retention are outside Scrigno’s control. We do not promise that the host keeps no connection logs.

This is the app’s only network use. Downloads begin only when you choose to download a voice model. Models can be downloaded again if you choose to replace or reinstall them. After installation, voice typing works offline. Notes and secure records work offline whether or not you use voice typing.

Scanning cards and documents (optional)

Scrigno uses a separate camera app provided through Android to photograph a page or card. Recognition and field extraction run on your phone. You review the proposed details and choose whether to save them. The local recogniser may read any text visible in the photo; the field parser excludes CVV/CVC security codes, PINs and one-time codes from the proposed fields. You may separately enter supported protected PIN and card-security-code fields yourself.

Scrigno uses a temporary camera file and cleans it up after reading it, or on cancellation, locking and subsequent startup cleanup. The photo and cropped page are held temporarily in memory during cropping and review, then released on cancellation, locking or expiry. A processed copy is encrypted in the vault only if you choose to keep the photo and save the record or page. Flash storage may retain traces, and the separate camera app may retain its own copy outside Scrigno’s control. Scrigno does not send the photo or recognised text to a server.

Permissions

Scrigno requests the fingerprint permissions Android requires. It requests microphone permission only when you tap the microphone for voice typing. It includes internet permission only for the optional voice-model downloads described above. Scrigno does not request location, contacts, camera, broad storage or notification permission. Photos and files are accessed through Android’s camera and file-picker interfaces, which grant access to the chosen item.

Deleting your data

You can delete content inside Scrigno. Notes moved to Recently Deleted can be restored until they are deleted permanently; notes older than 30 days are removed during the app’s next cleanup when you unlock it. Other deletion actions explain when they are permanent. Uninstalling Scrigno removes its app-local vault and downloaded models. Backup files and recovery-phrase files saved elsewhere remain there until you delete them. Copies you share or paste into other apps are outside Scrigno’s control.

Children

Scrigno is not intended for children under 18. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s information through the app.
